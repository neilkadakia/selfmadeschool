Salary Negotiation

The scariest 30 seconds of your career are worth more than most years of work.

Here’s the math nobody shows you: raises compound. A $10,000 higher starting salary, with normal percentage raises on top, is easily $100,000+ over a decade, from one conversation you were too polite to have.

Real talk

The company negotiated. Their first number was a negotiation move. You’re not starting the game, you’re just finally playing it.

Worked example

Anika gets offered $85,000. She says the four sentences with $97,000 as her number, then stops talking. The recruiter comes back at $92,000 plus a signing bonus.

10–15% a normal counter above the initial offer. High enough to matter, low enough that nobody blinks.

Knowledge check: option states

You said your number. The recruiter goes quiet. You should…

AImmediately soften: “but the current offer is fine too!”
BFill the silence with more reasons
CLet the silence sit. It’s part of the negotiation
Right. Whoever fills the silence first gives ground. You made a reasonable ask; the discomfort is doing the work. Let it.
