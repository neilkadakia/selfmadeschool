Office Hours

Questions everyone asks.

Is it actually free?

The 13th Grade is free — every module, no trial clock, no card required. The book is the only thing that costs money, and the course never requires it.

Do I need the book to take the course?

No — the course stands on its own. But it's highly recommended: the units are bite-size versions of the concepts, and the book breaks the same ideas down in much more detail. Course to get moving, book to go deep.

How long does it take?

24 units at your own pace. Most people do a unit a week — the modules are 10 minutes, and the homework is your real life.

Is this financial advice?

No. It's financial education — plain-English explanations so you can make your own calls.

Is this an actual school?

Deliberately not. Non-academic: no accreditation, no degrees, no transcripts, nothing to fail. The school theming is there because it's fun.
