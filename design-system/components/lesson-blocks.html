Nine Ways To Not Be A Wall Of Text

The blocks a written unit can carry beyond prose. Eight of them are generic: a unit fills one with its own numbers rather than commissioning a drawing, which is what makes thirty illustrated lessons possible.

  • A block that carries a tone is a solid fill with ink text, the way the Read / Watch / Do panels and the syllabus cards are. Never a tinted tile with a coloured band down one edge.
  • Reference material inverts to cream and reads as a document: a receipt, a table.
  • Everything else has no container. Steps, timelines, bars and scales are drawn straight onto the page with a spine, a dot or a bar.
  • Numbers are Bricolage and large. Labels are 12 to 13px uppercase at 0.18em, the site kicker.
  • Colour never travels alone. Every toned thing also carries a label, a number or a position, so the picture survives without it.

split

The same moment two ways. Amber is the trap, green is the move. Stacks on a phone, where each cell grows its own label.

receipt

A calculation worked in the open, ending in one number. The money workhorse, and the reason it inverts to cream is that it is a document.

bars

Quantities side by side, sized as a share of the largest. The author writes real numbers; the scale draws itself.

steps

A procedure the reader will actually run. The spine goes from each number to the next, so the last step ends the line.

Build it tonight

  1. 1Pick oneOne habit. Only one. Five new habits is how people end up with zero.
  2. 2Shrink itCut it until it takes two minutes and feels almost embarrassing.
  3. 3Anchor itAttach it to something that already happens every day. The old habit does the remembering.

flow

A sequence, or a cycle when it loops. The return leg is borders, not a path: an arrow inside a stretched viewBox arrives squashed.

timeline

Something over time. On a phone the stamp moves above the label and the dot keeps the left rail.

scale

A spectrum with the poles named. Marks sit on the track; their names are pills underneath, where they cannot collide.

table

Three or four things on the same rows. Two things belong in a split. Scrolls inside its own box, and the containment on that scroller is load-bearing.

art

Six drawings specific to Unit 01, named rather than free-form. Words inside a picture are DOM text, never SVG type, and the whole figure is announced as one image with one written sentence.
